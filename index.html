Gambit Rush Support

Gambit Rush is a quick-chess roguelike for iPhone: tiny boards, a ticking clock, and a run that grows harder as you go.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or feedback: bruceoio@hotmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap one of your cream pieces, then a highlighted square. Take the enemy King to clear the stage. The in-game "?" button and Settings > How to Play explain every piece.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does the game have ads or purchases? No. Version 1.0 has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. The game does not collect any data.
