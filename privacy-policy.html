Gambit Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Gambit Rush is a single-player chess game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

We do not collect personal data

The game does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (scores, coins, unlocks, settings and a run in progress) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves the device unless your operating system backs it up as part of your own device backup. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles personal data (for example ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
